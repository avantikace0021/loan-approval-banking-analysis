
LOAN APPROVAL & BANKING ANALYSIS - BUSINESS INSIGHTS

1. Dataset Overview:
- Total loan applications analyzed: 4269
- The dataset contains applicant income, loan amount, credit score, education,
  employment status, assets, and loan approval status.

2. Loan Approval Analysis:
- Approved loans: 2656
- Rejected loans: 1613
- Overall loan approval rate: approximately 62.22%

3. Income Analysis:
- Applicant income varies significantly across customers.
- Higher income applicants generally receive higher loan amounts.

4. Loan Amount Analysis:
- Loan amounts show variation based on applicant financial profile.
- Some applicants have significantly higher loan requirements.

5. Education Analysis:
- Education level affects loan approval patterns.
- Graduates show different approval trends compared to non-graduates.

6. Credit Score Analysis:
- CIBIL score is an important factor in loan approval decisions.
- Applicants with stronger credit scores are more likely to receive approval.

7. Property & Asset Analysis:
- Applicant assets help understand financial stability.
- Asset ownership can support better loan eligibility decisions.

8. Business Recommendations:
- Use credit score-based risk assessment.
- Review high-income applicants for higher-value loan opportunities.
- Improve approval decisions using applicant financial indicators.
- Monitor rejected applications to identify improvement areas.

Conclusion:
The analysis helps the financial institution improve loan approval decisions,
reduce risk, and identify potential customers.
